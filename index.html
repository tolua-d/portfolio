CASE STUDY / 001

Building and operating an HR platform

Full-time · Feb 2025 – present

Context

I contributed to the WorkFlowsHR MVP while at TechMaestro, then joined full-time in February 2025 as a core backend engineer on the live product.

What I built

GraphQL APIs (Graphene) for employee salary advance and leave management; an end-to-end recruitment module; an attendance clock in/out system; and a review-cycle engine with multi-rater feedback.

Payments & security

Paystack integration with webhook handling and Celery tasks for wallet, expense and salary advance disbursements. TOTP two-factor authentication, JWT and role-based access control.

Engineering considerations

Tenant-aware data access, reliable background processing, payment reconciliation and maintainable API contracts for the web dashboard.

Confidentiality: This case study describes my responsibilities and the system at a high level. It does not disclose proprietary source code, credentials or confidential customer information.
